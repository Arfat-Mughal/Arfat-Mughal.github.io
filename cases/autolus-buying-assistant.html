Autolus Buying Assistant

A conversational procurement demo that takes a single buying request through classification, financial coding, routing, approval, and an automatically raised purchase order.

The challenge

A requester should be able to explain what they need in plain language, without manually navigating procurement forms, selecting financial codes, estimating costs, or understanding the approval route. The system needed to use maintained business configuration instead of hardcoded agent decisions.

The approach

Conversation and decisions

Copilot Studio manages the conversation and Adaptive Cards. Dataverse supplies taxonomy, departments, cost centres, GL accounts, dimensions, travel policy, approval thresholds, suppliers, catalogue data, routing rules, and the audit trail.

Workflow orchestration

Four Power Automate flows provide coding and rate-card estimates, resolve the buying route and supplier, create the requisition, and escalate requests with an unknown value.

The delivered workflow

  1. Describe the purchase. The requester types one sentence.
  2. Classify and code it. The assistant identifies the category, cost centre, GL account, department, approver, and estimate when no value is given.
  3. Choose the route. The system selects preferred supplier, catalogue, P-card, sourcing, or escalation and prepares draft lines.
  4. Submit and approve. The draft becomes a requisition; approval cards are delivered in Teams and the demo raises a purchase order in Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations.

Result and next steps

The end-to-end demo passed all 18 planned tests. The intended follow-up work is to store requisitions in Dataverse rather than a live Finance environment, deliver approvals directly to each assigned approver, use signed-in requester data, and expose the newer configuration tables in the admin app.
